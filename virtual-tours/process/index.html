04 · Process

From your front door to every customer's phone.

Four stages, each with a clear handover. You keep running your business; we tell you exactly what we need from you, and when.

  1. 01CaptureOn site, in 360°
  2. 02CreateBuilt into one tour
  3. 03PublishOn your website, ready for phones
  4. 04PromoteWhere customers already look
CAPTURECREATEPUBLISHPROMOTE
01

Capture

We visit, walk the space with you and agree which areas matter most to your customers. Then we photograph each viewpoint in 360°, on site.

Your part

  • Access to the space at an agreed time
  • The areas you most want customers to see
  • The space prepared as a customer would find it

Our part

  • Planning the route and the viewpoints
  • Capturing each viewpoint in full 360°
  • Professional 360° capture
02

Create

We build the tour: each viewpoint becomes a seamless scene, scenes are linked so visitors move naturally from one to the next, and information is added where it helps a customer decide.

Your part

  • Details for hotspots, such as room sizes, menus or contact links
  • Feedback on the draft tour

Our part

  • Building the tour from the captured scenes
  • Linking viewpoints into a natural route
  • Adding information hotspots where they help
  • Tour construction
  • Interactive navigation
  • Information hotspots, where applicable
03

Publish

Your tour goes live with its own link and is placed on your website, then checked on phones as carefully as on computers, because that is where most people will open it.

Your part

  • Website access, or an introduction to whoever manages it

Our part

  • Embedding the tour on your website
  • Checking it works well on mobile
  • Website embedding
  • Mobile optimisation
04

Promote

A tour only helps if people find it. We help connect it to the places you already reach customers: your website, WhatsApp replies, social media and advertising.

Your part

  • The channels you use today and your priorities

Our part

  • Connecting the tour to those channels
  • Supporting digital integration
05What you receive

A finished tour, live and ready to share.

The exact areas and number of viewpoints are agreed in your proposal, so you know what is included before you commit.

06Questions

What owners usually ask us.

What exactly do we get at the end?

A live, interactive 360° tour of the areas agreed in your proposal, with linked viewpoints, information hotspots where they are useful, a link you can share and the tour embedded on your website.

How will customers open it?

From a link, in their normal web browser, on a phone, tablet or computer. There is nothing for them to install.

Where can we use the tour?

On your website, in WhatsApp replies to enquiries, on social media, in advertising and anywhere else you can share a link.

Do we need a website?

No. The tour works on its own link. If you do have a website, we embed it there too.

How is it priced?

Every space is different, so we quote per project after a short conversation about the areas you want to cover. You see what is included in the proposal before anything is agreed.

How do we start?

Tell us about your space using the project form. We review it, contact you to confirm the details and then send a proposal.
