03 · Industries

Wherever people need to picture the space first.

Different businesses, the same moment: a customer trying to imagine your space before they commit. Here is what a tour shows them in each case.

These are examples of where tours help. They are not a list of past clients or case studies.

  1. 01Hotels & lodges
  2. 02Restaurants
  3. 03Event venues
  4. 04Real estate
  5. 05Schools

01 / 07

Hotels, resorts & lodges

“Is the room really like the photos?”

Guests compare several places in a few minutes, and wide-angle photos start to look alike. The details that decide a booking, like the view, the bathroom or the walk to the pool, are the hardest to show in a gallery.

Why it mattersGuests who have already looked around book with fewer doubts, and arrive expecting exactly what they find.

What a tour shows them

  • Each room category, exactly as it is
  • The view from the room, not just the lobby
  • Facilities and the route between them

02 / 07

Restaurants

“Is it the right place for tonight?”

Atmosphere is what people are choosing, and it rarely survives a single photo. Diners want to know how the room feels, where they would sit and whether it suits the occasion.

Why it mattersDiners choose with more confidence when they have seen the room, especially for occasions that matter.

What a tour shows them

  • The mood and layout of the room
  • Private dining, terrace or garden areas
  • A menu or booking link, right from the scene
Get a tour for your restaurant

03 / 07

Event & conference venues

“Will our event fit, and how will it flow?”

Planners need to judge capacity, layout and access, often on behalf of a committee that will never visit. Photos of an empty hall rarely answer how a day will actually move through the building.

Why it mattersPlanners can shortlist you without a site visit, and share the space with everyone who has a say.

What a tour shows them

  • Halls, breakout rooms and outdoor spaces
  • Entrances, the stage area and the route between rooms
  • One link a planner can forward to everyone involved
Get a tour for your venue

04 / 07

Real estate & property

“Is it worth arranging a viewing?”

Viewings take time for agents and buyers alike, and many buyers, including those living abroad, cannot visit early in their search. A gallery shows the best rooms; buyers want to understand the whole home.

Why it mattersViewings go to people who have already seen the layout and still want to come.

What a tour shows them

  • Every room, in the order you would walk it
  • Layout, proportions and natural light
  • Shared spaces and the surroundings
Get a tour for your property

05 / 07

Schools & educational institutions

“Is this the right place for my child?”

Families choosing a school, especially a boarding school, want to see where their child will learn and live. Not every family can visit before applying, and a brochure only goes so far.

Why it mattersFamilies far away can take a proper look before they apply or visit.

What a tour shows them

  • Classrooms, laboratories and the library
  • Boarding, dining and common rooms
  • Sports grounds and outdoor spaces
Get a tour for your school

06 / 07

Tourism attractions & experiences

“Is it worth the journey?”

Visitors plan trips around places they can picture. If an experience is hard to imagine, it is easy to leave off the itinerary, especially when it means a long drive.

Why it mattersA place people can picture is a place they can plan a trip around.

What a tour shows them

  • The arrival and the main highlights
  • Accommodation and facilities on site
  • Enough of the place to plan the visit
Get a tour for your attraction

07 / 07

Showrooms & commercial spaces

“Is it worth coming in?”

Customers want to see the range, the scale and the finish before they make the trip, and many of them are browsing outside your opening hours.

Why it mattersVisitors arrive knowing what they came to see, and ask better questions when they do.

What a tour shows them

  • Products in place, at their real scale
  • The full floor, open around the clock
  • Enquiry links beside what they are looking at
